## What it is

The public Agent Skills package for Relux Works. Installing it teaches any coding agent
(Claude Code, Cursor, OpenClaw, Hermes, and others) how to look up our services and
pricing and submit a real project inquiry on the user's behalf — a human replies within
one business day.

## Why it matters

It is the client side of the same agent-native loop we practice on ourselves: a skill
discoverable in the major registries (ClawHub, Hermes taps, HermesHub/ARD) that resolves
to a live MCP server. An agent finds the skill, reads the facts, and can act — no human
on our side of the pipe until the lead lands in Telegram.

## Who it is for

Agents and the founders behind them who would rather have their assistant talk to ours
than fill out a form.
